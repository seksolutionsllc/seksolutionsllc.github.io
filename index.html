Services

Services in detail

Every engagement is owner-delivered. The person who bids the work is the person who teaches, audits, and writes it.

Safety Training Delivery

Primary NAICS 611430 — Professional & Management Development Training

On-site, owner-delivered training, scheduled to fit facility shift patterns — day, evening, and night sessions available, so crews train on their own shift instead of being pulled off it. Courses are built for industrial and government facilities where the work is physical, the hazards are real, and attendance and completion have to be documented.

  • Fall protection and ladder safety
  • Lockout/tagout (LOTO) awareness and procedure discipline
  • Aerial / scissor lift safety
  • Forklift and powered industrial truck safety awareness
  • Fire safety and extinguisher basics
  • Job hazard analysis and pre-task briefings
  • Incident-investigation method — root-cause documentation from camera and record review

Safety Consulting

NAICS 541690 — Other Scientific & Technical Consulting Services

Practical safety consulting grounded in operating experience — not template programs. SEK Solutions helps facilities find the gaps between the written program and what actually happens on the floor, then close them.

  • Safety audits — facility and program audits with findings documented in plain, actionable terms
  • Program development — building or rebuilding safety programs, procedures, and briefing disciplines that crews will actually use
  • Compliance support — help aligning day-to-day practice with the requirements the facility operates under

E-Learning & Course Development

NAICS 611710 / 512110 — Educational Support Services / Video Production

When training has to scale beyond the classroom — across shifts, sites, or a distributed workforce — SEK Solutions builds it as content. Subject-matter depth comes first: the material is written by an operator, then produced as training your people can complete anywhere.

  • E-learning production — complete, ready-to-deliver training content
  • Curriculum & course writing — structured courses built from your procedures and requirements
  • Instructional design — learning objectives, assessments, and materials designed for retention, not just completion

Security Consulting

Assessments & program reviews

Advisory security consulting informed by a Military Police background and years of securing a critical-infrastructure rail terminal: an outside set of experienced eyes on your posture, procedures, and weak points.

  • Security assessments — structured reviews of facilities, procedures, and vulnerabilities
  • Program reviews — evaluation of existing security programs with documented recommendations
Scope note SEK Solutions provides assessments and program reviews only. We do not provide guard services, patrol, or security staffing.
